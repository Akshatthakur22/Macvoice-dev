Voice notes are convenient to send and annoying to receive. Dictating gives you the speed of talking and gives the other person a text they can read in two seconds.

## Dictate into WhatsApp on Mac

1. Open the **WhatsApp desktop app** or **WhatsApp Web**.
2. Click into the message box.
3. Start your dictation tool (Apple Dictation: Microphone key or your shortcut; [MacVoice](/): Control + Option + Space).
4. Speak the message. Stop. Read it. Press Return to send.

## The "new line" problem

In WhatsApp, **Return sends the message**. If your dictation tool turns "new line" into Return, you'll send half a message. Your options:

- Don't say "new line"; keep messages to one paragraph.
- Press **Shift + Return** yourself between paragraphs.
- Use a tool that's chat-aware. MacVoice recognises WhatsApp (app and web) and sends Shift+Return for "new line". The full explanation is in [how to dictate in Slack without sending](/blog/dictate-in-slack-without-sending); WhatsApp works the same way.

## Make messages read naturally

- **Polished mode** in MacVoice removes "um" and "uh", so your message doesn't read like a transcript.
- Say **"question mark"** or **"exclamation mark"** for tone; or let auto-punctuation guess.
- **Emoji:** Apple Dictation can insert emoji in some languages ("heart emoji"). Otherwise, add them with the keyboard (Control + Command + Space opens the emoji picker).

## Long messages without the wall of text

Dictation makes long messages easy, which can mean very long messages. Some habits keep them readable:

- **Lead with the point:** "Can we move dinner to 8? Here's why…"
- **Use "bullet point"** (in MacVoice) for options or lists: "bullet point Friday at 8 bullet point Saturday at 7".
- **Split topics** into separate messages instead of one huge block.
- **Read it once** before sending; names and times are where errors hide.

## Group chats and work chats

The same approach works in WhatsApp groups, and in Slack, Teams, Telegram and Messages. If you use WhatsApp for work, the [Slack dictation guide](/blog/dictate-in-slack-without-sending) covers more patterns for team updates.

## Privacy

WhatsApp encrypts messages end to end, but your *dictation tool* is a separate question: some send your voice to a server to transcribe it. If you dictate personal messages, choose on-device dictation. See [is Mac dictation private?](/blog/is-mac-dictation-private)
