Wispr Flow is one of the best-known AI dictation apps, and for good reason: it's fast, polished and its AI rewriting is genuinely useful. People look for alternatives for three main reasons: **privacy, price and offline use.**

*Facts checked November 2026; check vendors for current pricing.*

## What Wispr Flow does well

- AI turns natural speech into clean, well-formatted text, adjusting to the app you're in.
- Works across **Mac, Windows and iPhone**.
- Free tier (about **2,000 words per week** on desktop); **Pro around $12/month billed yearly (~$144/year)**.

## Why people switch

- **Cloud processing:** audio is sent to servers. Some workplaces don't allow that ([what happens to your voice](/blog/what-happens-to-your-voice-cloud-dictation)).
- **Needs internet.**
- **Subscription** cost over years.
- **Clipboard-based insertion:** Wispr pastes via the clipboard and then restores it, which is well handled but can show up in clipboard managers or fail where paste is blocked ([details](/blog/dictation-without-clipboard-mac)).

## The alternatives

| | Privacy | Price | AI rewriting | Text while you talk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Apple Dictation** | On-device (many languages) | Free | No | Yes |
| **MacVoice** | On-device | Free | No (rule-based cleanup) | Yes |
| **VoiceInk** | Local models | One-time, from ~$25 | Optional (bring your own AI) | No |
| **Superwhisper** | Local or cloud | Free tier; ~$85/yr; $249.99 lifetime | Yes (modes) | No |

### MacVoice: closest for "fast and private"

[MacVoice](/) runs on Apple's on-device engine, so nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and it's **free**. It types *while* you talk, removes fillers instantly, follows "new line", "bullet point" and "scratch that", sends Shift+Return in Slack and WhatsApp, and never touches your clipboard.

What you give up vs Wispr: **AI rewriting** (MacVoice cleans up, it doesn't rephrase), **Windows/iPhone apps**, and it needs **macOS 26+**.

### VoiceInk: open source

Local Whisper/Parakeet models, a one-time price, and code you can inspect. You manage model downloads. Apple silicon only.

### Superwhisper: most flexible

Choose local or cloud models and create custom AI modes for different tasks. Higher price; local models need downloads.

### Apple Dictation: free baseline

Try it first; it might be enough ([guide](/blog/how-to-use-dictation-on-mac)).

## Which to pick

- **Want Wispr-style AI polish, but cheaper/local:** Superwhisper or VoiceInk with local models.
- **Want privacy, instant typing and no subscription:** MacVoice.
- **Want free:** Apple Dictation.
