Dictation on iPhone is one of the most-used features nobody thinks about. A few tips make it far better.

## Turn it on

**Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation.** Your iPhone may download language files.

## Use it

1. Tap into any text field.
2. Tap the **microphone** on the keyboard.
3. Talk. Tap the microphone again (or stop talking) to finish.

## Punctuation and formatting

- In supported languages, **commas, periods and question marks are added automatically**. Turn this off in Settings → General → Keyboard → *Auto-Punctuation*.
- Say marks out loud: "comma", "period", "question mark", "exclamation point".
- Say **"new line"** or **"new paragraph"**.

## Emoji

Say the emoji name plus "emoji": "heart emoji", "thumbs up emoji". Works in some languages; obvious names work best.

## Type while you dictate

Since iOS 16, the keyboard stays open during dictation. Tap a word to fix it or type a correction **without stopping**.

## Editing by voice

Recent iPhones support voice editing commands in some languages, such as selecting and deleting text. For full voice control of the phone, see **Settings → Accessibility → Voice Control**.

## Tips

- **Hold the phone near your mouth** in noisy places, or use AirPods.
- **Speak in full phrases**; pause between sentences ([accuracy tips](/blog/speech-to-text-accuracy-tips)).
- **Check names** before sending; they're the most common errors.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No microphone on the keyboard | Turn on Enable Dictation; check Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions isn't blocking it |
| Dictation stops quickly | It stops after you pause; keep talking, or tap the mic again to resume |
| Wrong language | Add the language under Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards; switch with the globe key before dictating |
| Words keep coming out wrong | Move closer to the mic, reduce background noise, and speak in full phrases |
| Unwanted punctuation | Turn off Auto-Punctuation and say punctuation yourself |

## Dictation vs voice messages

In Messages and WhatsApp you can either dictate text or send a voice message. Dictated text is quicker for the other person to read, searchable later, and readable in meetings; voice messages carry tone. For anything with details (addresses, times, lists) text is kinder to the reader.

## Privacy

On recent iPhones, dictation in many languages is processed on-device. Apple also asks whether to share audio recordings to improve Siri and Dictation; you can turn that off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. More on how this works on the Mac side in [is Mac dictation private?](/blog/is-mac-dictation-private)

## iPhone and Mac

iPhone dictation is great for messages on the go. At your desk, the same habits transfer to the Mac: Apple Dictation ([Mac guide](/blog/how-to-use-dictation-on-mac)) or a dictation app like [MacVoice](/) for cleanup, custom words and chat-safe line breaks.
