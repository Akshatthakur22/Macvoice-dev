Word has its own dictation feature, and your Mac has others. Here's how they compare.

## Using Word's Dictate

1. Open a document in **Word for Mac**.
2. On the **Home** tab, click **Dictate** (the microphone).
3. Allow microphone access if asked.
4. Talk. Say punctuation like "comma" and "new line"; click Dictate again to stop.

The Dictate toolbar lets you pick the language and turn auto-punctuation on or off.

## What it needs

- A **Microsoft 365** subscription and being **signed in**.
- An **internet connection**: Word's dictation is processed by Microsoft's cloud service.
- A supported language (newer "preview" languages may be less accurate).

## Common problems

| Problem | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Dictate button greyed out | Offline or not signed in |
| Button missing | Older Word version or not a Microsoft 365 licence |
| Nothing typed | Microphone permission for Word in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone |
| Wrong language | Change the language in the Dictate toolbar |

## Alternatives that work in Word

**Apple Dictation** works in Word like in any app: click in the document and press your shortcut. On many Macs and languages it runs on-device ([check yours](/blog/does-mac-dictation-work-offline)).

**[MacVoice](/)** types into Word as keyboard input, with "um" removal, "new paragraph" and "bullet point" commands, and custom words for names and jargon. It doesn't need Microsoft 365 or an internet connection, and it works the same in Pages, Google Docs and email.

## Tips for dictating long Word documents

- **Type your headings first,** then dictate under each one. Word's navigation pane makes it easy to jump between sections.
- **Use "new paragraph"** generously; it's easier to merge paragraphs than split them later.
- **Turn on Track Changes** when editing a dictated draft someone else will review, so cleanup edits are visible.
- **Proofread with Read Aloud** (Review tab): hearing your text is a fast way to spot misheard words.
- **Add names and jargon** to your dictation tool's [custom words](/blog/add-custom-words-mac-dictation), especially client names and technical terms.

For the drafting method itself, see [how to write a first draft by voice](/blog/write-first-draft-by-voice).

## Which should you use?

- **Already pay for Microsoft 365 and only write in Word?** Word's Dictate is fine.
- **Write in many apps, or work with confidential documents?** Use something that works everywhere and keeps audio on your Mac. See [dictating confidential information](/blog/dictating-confidential-information).
