For years, Dragon was *the* dictation software, and many Mac users relied on Dragon Dictate and later Dragon Professional for Mac. In **2018 Nuance discontinued Dragon for Mac**, focusing on Windows. (Nuance has since been acquired by Microsoft.) If you're still searching for "Dragon for Mac", here's what replaced it.

## What Dragon users need

Dragon users usually relied on three things:

1. **Accurate dictation**, including specialised vocabulary.
2. **Voice commands** to correct and format text.
3. **Hands-free control** of the computer.

## The good news: no more voice training

Dragon asked you to train it on your voice. Modern neural speech models are trained on enormous amounts of speech and **work well immediately**. You still benefit from adding names and jargon ([custom words](/blog/add-custom-words-mac-dictation)).

## Alternatives by need

| Dragon feature | Mac replacement |
|---|---|
| Hands-free computer control | **Voice Control** (built in, free) |
| Correction commands ("select…", "delete that") | **Voice Control** |
| Custom vocabulary | Voice Control Vocabulary; dictation apps' custom words |
| Fast, accurate dictation in any app | Apple Dictation; dictation apps |
| Specialised medical/legal workflows | Dedicated professional products (often cloud-based) |

### Voice Control (free, built in)

The closest equivalent to Dragon's command-and-control. Say "show numbers" to click anything, dictate and edit by voice, and add vocabulary. [Voice Control vs Dictation](/blog/voice-control-vs-dictation-mac).

### Apple Dictation (free)

Quick voice typing anywhere, often on-device ([guide](/blog/how-to-use-dictation-on-mac)).

### Modern dictation apps

- **[MacVoice](/)** (on-device, $9 once): types as you speak, removes fillers, "scratch that", custom words and replacements. Writing-focused; no click commands.
- **Wispr Flow** (cloud, subscription): AI-polished output.
- **Superwhisper / VoiceInk** (local Whisper models): flexible, more languages.

Full comparison: [best dictation apps for Mac](/blog/best-dictation-apps-for-mac).

## A Dragon-like setup on Mac

Many former Dragon users combine:

1. **Voice Control** for navigation and clicking,
2. a **dictation app** for long-form writing with cleanup,
3. a good **headset microphone** ([mic guide](/blog/best-microphone-for-dictation)).

If you dictate professionally with confidential material, read [dictating confidential information](/blog/dictating-confidential-information) before choosing.
