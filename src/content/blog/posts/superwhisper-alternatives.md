Superwhisper is a favourite among Mac power users: choose your model, build custom AI modes, run locally or in the cloud. If you're looking for something **cheaper, lighter or simpler**, here are the options.

*Facts checked November 2026; prices change, so check vendors.*

## What Superwhisper offers

- **Local models** (Whisper, Parakeet) and **cloud models**.
- **Custom modes** that use AI to reformat text for emails, notes, code and more.
- Mac, iPhone and (newer) Windows apps, with one licence covering Mac and Windows.
- Free tier; **Pro around $8.49/month or $84.99/year; lifetime $249.99**.

## Why people look elsewhere

- **Price** for occasional users.
- **Model downloads** (hundreds of MB to GBs for the larger local models).
- **Waiting for text:** like most Whisper apps it transcribes after you stop speaking.
- **More settings than they need.**

## Alternatives

| | Engine | Price | Text while you talk | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MacVoice** | Apple on-device | $9 once | Yes | Under 1 MB (system model) |
| **VoiceInk** | Local Whisper/Parakeet | One-time, from ~$25 | No | App + models |
| **Wispr Flow** | Cloud | Free tier; ~$144/yr | No | Standard app |
| **Apple Dictation** | Apple | Free | Yes | Built in |

### MacVoice: simple, tiny, private

[MacVoice](/) skips model management entirely: it uses Apple's on-device speech engine that macOS manages. You press a shortcut and text streams in as you speak, with instant filler removal, voice commands, custom words, and chat- and terminal-aware typing. **$9 once.**

What you give up vs Superwhisper: **model choice, AI modes, iPhone and Windows apps, broad language support**; it requires **macOS 26**. If you rely on custom AI modes, stay with Superwhisper.

### VoiceInk: open-source Whisper

Closest in spirit to Superwhisper's local mode, with a lower one-time price and public source code.

### Wispr Flow: cloud polish

If what you love is AI-polished output and you don't mind the cloud. See [Wispr Flow alternatives](/blog/wispr-flow-alternatives) for the trade-offs.

## Choosing

- Want **power and flexibility** → stay with Superwhisper.
- Want **cheap, fast, private, no setup** → MacVoice.
- Want **open source** → VoiceInk.
