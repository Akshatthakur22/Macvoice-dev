## Apple Dictation

**Best for:** everyone, as a starting point. Free, works everywhere, often on-device. **Missing:** filler removal, custom words (outside Voice Control), chat-safe line breaks. [Guide](/blog/how-to-use-dictation-on-mac).

## MacVoice (ours)

**Best for:** people who write a lot in many apps and want privacy without a subscription. Built on Apple's on-device engine, types as you talk, removes fillers instantly, "scratch that", custom words, chat- and terminal-aware, never touches the clipboard, under 1 MB, $9 once.

**Not for you if:** you're on macOS 15 or earlier, need a language Apple's engine doesn't support yet, or want AI to rewrite your tone. Full English cleanup only.

## Wispr Flow

**Best for:** people who want AI to turn rambling speech into polished text, across Mac, Windows and iPhone. **Trade-offs:** cloud processing (needs internet, audio leaves your device), subscription, account required. [Wispr Flow alternatives](/blog/wispr-flow-alternatives).

## Superwhisper

**Best for:** power users who want to choose models (local or cloud) and build custom AI "modes". **Trade-offs:** larger downloads for local models, higher price. [Superwhisper alternatives](/blog/superwhisper-alternatives).

## VoiceInk

**Best for:** people who want open source, local models and a one-time price. **Trade-offs:** Apple silicon only; you choose and download models.

## Coming from Dragon?

Dragon for Mac was discontinued in 2018. See [Dragon dictation alternatives for Mac](/blog/dragon-dictation-mac-alternatives) for how Voice Control and modern apps replace it.

## How to choose in 10 minutes

1. Try **Apple Dictation** for a day. If it's enough, you're done.
2. Decide: **on-device or cloud?** ([the trade-offs](/blog/on-device-vs-cloud-speech-recognition))
3. Decide: **do you want AI rewriting** or faithful, cleaned-up text?
4. Test your shortlist with the same 100-word paragraph, including your names and jargon ([how to measure](/blog/word-error-rate-explained)).
