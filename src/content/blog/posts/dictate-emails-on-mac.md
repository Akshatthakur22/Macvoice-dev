Email is the perfect place to start voice typing: it's mostly plain prose, it's repetitive, and you probably write a lot of it.

## Your options by app

| Email app | Built-in dictation | Other ways |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Mail | Uses Apple Dictation | Any Mac dictation app |
| Gmail (in a browser) | None on desktop | Apple Dictation or a dictation app |
| Outlook for Mac | **Dictate** button (Microsoft 365, internet needed) | Apple Dictation or a dictation app |

Because dictation apps type into whatever text box is focused, they work the same in all three.

## A simple way to dictate an email

1. **Fill in the recipient yourself.** Pick from autocomplete; addresses are the hardest thing for speech engines.
2. **Click into the subject** and dictate a short subject.
3. **Click into the body** and dictate in this order:
   - Greeting: "Hi Sam comma new paragraph"
   - One sentence on why you're writing
   - The details, one idea per paragraph ("new paragraph" between them)
   - The ask: what you need and by when
   - Sign-off
4. **Stop and read it once** before sending, especially names, numbers and dates.

Speaking in **whole sentences** gives better punctuation than stopping mid-thought. See [how to get better at dictation](/blog/how-to-get-better-at-dictation).

## Speed up the boring parts

Most of us type the same snippets dozens of times a week: sign-offs, our phone number, a meeting link. In [MacVoice](/), add **Replacements** (Words tab):

```
sign off => Best regards,\nPriya
my number => +91 98765 43210
my calendar => https://cal.example.com/priya
```

Say "sign off" and the full signature appears.

## Fixing common email dictation mistakes

- **"um" and "uh" in the text:** Apple Dictation types hesitations. Polished mode in MacVoice removes them ([why that matters](/blog/why-dictation-removes-filler-words)).
- **Names spelled wrong:** add them as custom words ([how](/blog/add-custom-words-mac-dictation)).
- **One giant paragraph:** say "new paragraph" between ideas.
- **Wrong tone:** dictation is literal. Speak the way you'd write: "I wanted to check in" rather than "yeah so basically".

## Writing longer documents?

The same approach works in [Google Docs](/blog/voice-typing-google-docs-mac) and [Microsoft Word](/blog/dictate-in-microsoft-word-mac), each of which also has its own dictation feature with different limits.

## Privacy note for work email

If your emails contain confidential client or patient information, check where your dictation tool sends audio. Built-in Dictation can be on-device; Outlook's Dictate and cloud dictation apps process audio on servers. See [dictating confidential information](/blog/dictating-confidential-information).
