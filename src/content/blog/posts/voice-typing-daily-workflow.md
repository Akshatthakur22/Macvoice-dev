What does a day of voice typing actually look like? Here's a realistic routine that mixes voice and keyboard, with the reasoning behind each choice.

## Morning: email

- **Long replies:** dictate. Click into the reply, shortcut, talk, stop, skim, send. ([Email guide](/blog/dictate-emails-on-mac))
- **One-word replies** ("Thanks!", "Approved"): type them. Faster than starting dictation.
- **Boilerplate:** use replacements ("sign off" → your signature).

## Midday: Slack and Teams

- **Explanations, updates, feedback:** dictate. Use "bullet point" for lists.
- **Avoid early sends:** a chat-aware tool turns "new line" into Shift+Return ([how](/blog/dictate-in-slack-without-sending)).
- **Quick emoji reactions:** keyboard or mouse.

## Afternoon: documents

- **Outline by keyboard, draft by voice, edit by keyboard** ([method](/blog/write-first-draft-by-voice)).
- Google Docs, Word, Notion: same shortcut everywhere.

## Throughout: AI prompts

Dictate detailed prompts to ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor. Spoken prompts are longer and get better answers ([guide](/blog/dictate-prompts-chatgpt-claude-cursor)).

## End of day: notes and journal

Capture tomorrow's to-dos and thoughts by voice in ten seconds each ([notes guide](/blog/dictate-in-notion-and-obsidian)).

## A sample day in numbers

Here's an illustrative day for someone who writes a lot:

| Task | Typical volume | By voice? |
|---|---|---|
| Email replies | 10 × 80 words | Yes, except one-liners |
| Slack/Teams | 30 messages, ~25 words | Longer ones |
| Document drafting | 1,000 words | Draft by voice, edit by keyboard |
| AI prompts | 10 × 60 words | Yes |
| Notes | 15 quick captures | Yes |

That's roughly 3,000 words of prose. At around 40 words per minute typed, that's over an hour of typing; speaking it at even double that speed gives back a meaningful chunk of the day ([the research](/blog/speaking-vs-typing-speed)).

## Where the keyboard still wins

- Short edits and corrections
- Keyboard shortcuts and navigation
- Code and anything symbol-heavy
- Quiet spaces and meetings ([open-office tips](/blog/dictating-in-an-open-office))

## Setup that makes it smooth

1. **One global shortcut** for every app. [MacVoice](/) uses Control + Option + Space, and you press it again to stop.
2. **Custom words** for names, products, jargon.
3. **Replacements** for signature, email, links.
4. **A good mic** if you're not alone ([mic guide](/blog/best-microphone-for-dictation)).

## Track the win

For a week, note how long your longest writing tasks take. Most people find voice typing pays off most on the tasks they used to put off because they were tedious to type. Background: [speaking vs typing speed](/blog/speaking-vs-typing-speed).
