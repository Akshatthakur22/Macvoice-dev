The first time most people dictate, they stop mid-sentence, fix a word, lose their thread and give up. Dictation isn't harder than typing; it's a different skill. These habits get you there.

## 1. Think, then speak

Typing lets you compose as you go. Speaking works better if you **form the sentence first**, then say it in one go. A one-second pause before you start is fine.

## 2. Speak in sentences, pause between them

Pauses are used to place punctuation. Pause **between** sentences, not in the middle ([auto-punctuation](/blog/mac-dictation-auto-punctuation)).

## 3. Don't edit while you talk

Finish the paragraph, *then* fix. Constant fixing is what makes dictation feel slow ([first-draft method](/blog/write-first-draft-by-voice)).

## 4. Learn five commands

You don't need fifty. Start with: **new line, new paragraph, comma, question mark, scratch that** ([cheat sheet](/blog/mac-voice-typing-commands)).

## 5. Replace "um" with silence

A pause costs nothing; an "um" may end up in your text. Good tools remove fillers anyway ([how](/blog/why-dictation-removes-filler-words)), but silence is cleaner.

## 6. Teach it your words on day one

The fastest way to stop being annoyed: add your top 20 names and terms as [custom words](/blog/add-custom-words-mac-dictation).

## 7. Start with one task

Pick the task you do most, usually **email or Slack**, and dictate only that for a week. Then add documents, then notes, then AI prompts.

## 8. Get the setup right

A close microphone and a quiet-ish room matter more than any technique ([accuracy tips](/blog/speech-to-text-accuracy-tips)).

## 9. Talk like you write, not like you chat

"Hey so basically I was thinking maybe we could…" transcribes perfectly and reads badly. Aim for how you'd write it: "I'd like to suggest…". This comes with practice.

## 10. Keep the keyboard for what it's good at

Short edits, shortcuts and code are still faster by hand. The goal is the right tool for each moment, not 100% voice.

## A one-week plan

| Day | Practice |
|---|---|
| 1 | Set up shortcut, add custom words, dictate 5 emails |
| 2–3 | All emails by voice; use "new paragraph" and "comma" |
| 4 | Add Slack/Teams replies |
| 5 | Dictate one longer document section using the outline method |
| 6–7 | Add AI prompts or notes; try "scratch that" instead of reaching for the keyboard |
