Most dictation errors aren't the software's fault alone: they come from audio, habits and settings you can change in minutes.

## Audio

**1. Get the microphone closer.** Distance matters more than price. AirPods, a headset, or a desk mic near your mouth beat a laptop mic across the desk. [Microphone guide](/blog/best-microphone-for-dictation).

**2. Make sure the right mic is selected.** On Mac: System Settings → Sound → Input, and Keyboard → Dictation → Microphone source. A webcam mic on the other side of the room is a classic culprit.

**3. Reduce echo and noise.** Soft furnishings help; bare rooms echo. Close the window, turn off the fan.

## How you speak

**4. Speak in full phrases.** Models use context. "Send the report to Priya by Friday" beats "send… the… report…".

**5. Natural pace, clear endings.** Don't rush, but don't over-enunciate either.

**6. Pause between sentences, not inside them.** Pauses help decide where periods go ([auto-punctuation](/blog/mac-dictation-auto-punctuation)).

**7. Think, then talk.** If you need to think, stop dictating, think, then start again. "Ums" and long pauses cause errors. (A tool that [removes fillers](/blog/why-dictation-removes-filler-words) helps when they slip in.)

## Settings

**8. Pick your regional language.** English (India), (UK), (Australia) and others are trained on those accents ([accent tips](/blog/voice-typing-with-an-accent)).

**9. Add custom words.** Names and jargon are the most common errors. Add them once ([how](/blog/add-custom-words-mac-dictation)).

**10. Use replacements for repeat offenders.** If a word is always wrong the same way, map it.

## Habits

**11. Edit after, not during.** Fixing every word as you go breaks your flow and slows you down. Dictate a section, then fix ([method](/blog/write-first-draft-by-voice)).

**12. Measure your own accuracy.** Read a typical 100-word paragraph and count fixes. Try again after changing one thing. That's your personal [word error rate](/blog/word-error-rate-explained).

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Mic close to mouth, right mic selected
- [ ] Quiet-ish room
- [ ] Regional language set
- [ ] Top 20 names and terms added as custom words
- [ ] Speaking in phrases, pausing between sentences
- [ ] Editing after each section, not during
